04 / YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Your perspective.
Our next possibility.

Every project starts somewhere. A line of code, a clearer sentence, a fresh idea. There’s a place for your piece.

START SMALL. BUILD TOGETHER.

A little curiosity goes a long way.

You don’t need the whole answer. Start with one useful change, one clearer sentence, or one good question.

Find your first step
CODEImprove the code.

Start by understanding a project, then make one small improvement.

  1. Explore the repository and read its README.
  2. Check the contribution guide before making changes.
  3. Open a focused pull request and explain your change.
Read the contribution guide
DOCUMENTATIONMake the docs clearer.

A clearer sentence can be as useful as a clever function.

  1. Look for unclear instructions or missing examples.
  2. Check the Arabic and English experience.
  3. Suggest a change in an issue or a pull request.
Suggest an improvement
IDEAS & FEEDBACKShare an idea or a problem.

Found a rough edge? Have an idea? Help us see what we’ve missed.

  1. Search existing issues to avoid duplicates.
  2. Describe what happened and what you expected.
  3. Add an example or steps to reproduce if you can.
Explore the issues
